## Goal

Describe a generic role and the outcome it needs using a fictional scenario.

## Observation and evidence

Describe the friction or lesson. Distinguish reported behavior, an observed outcome,
and a reproduction on invented input. Use only synthetic examples. Exclude actual
product names, people, paths, identifiers, logs, usage counts, and project data.

## Workaround and impact

Explain the extra step or practice and when it matters.

## Acceptance scenario

Before finishing, read the draft as a maintainer who has never seen the session.
Use the question for the feedback type to check the whole draft.

- **Defect** — Could the maintainer try the minimal steps on invented input and tell the expected result from the reported or reproduced failure?
- **Feature request** — Could they identify the awkward step, its consequence, and what the user could do if the request were implemented?
- **Guidance gap** — Could they identify the decision the reader faced, what guidance was missing, and what the reader should do?
- **Workflow lesson** — Could they tell when the practice applies, how to follow it, and what outcome to look for?

State any missing evidence that matters to the maintainer’s next step.
